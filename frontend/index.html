    About Trnscrpt

    Trnscrpt is a simple online tool that converts supported publicly accessible video content into searchable text. Paste a link, and get a timestamped transcript in moments — no account required.

    What you get

    • No account required
    • Multilingual transcription with automatic language detection
    • Timestamped transcripts, down to the segment
    • Export as TXT, SRT, or PDF
    • Optional translation into 12+ languages
    • Optional AI-generated summaries

    Transcription quality depends on audio clarity, accents, and background noise. Trnscrpt does its best but doesn't claim perfect accuracy.

    How It Works

    1. Paste your video link

    Copy a public video URL from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or Pinterest and paste it into the box on the homepage.

    2. Generate transcript

    Trnscrpt detects the platform, extracts the audio, detects the spoken language, and transcribes it with timestamps.

    3. Copy or download

    Copy the text directly, or export it as TXT, SRT, or PDF. Translate it into another language or generate a short summary first if you like.

    Privacy Policy

    What we collect

    We process the video URL you submit, and temporarily store extracted audio and the resulting transcript while your job is processed. We log IP addresses briefly for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

    Retention

    Temporary media files are deleted after processing. Transcripts and job records expire automatically after a short retention window.

    Third parties

    We use third-party speech-to-text and translation services to generate transcripts, and may display third-party advertising, which can set cookies.

    Your choices

    Since no account is required, we hold minimal personal data. Contact us if you'd like data associated with a specific job removed.
